# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, be constructive, assume good intent, and keep the
space welcoming for contributors of every background and experience level.
Harassment, personal attacks, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

Reports of unacceptable behavior: **sandeepbazar@gmail.com**. All reports are
reviewed and handled confidentially.
