## What counts as adoption

- Running the server against a real or lab Open Cluster Management / ACM-MCE fleet.
- Building on the guardrail pattern (propose, policy-admit, human-approve, apply,
  audit) in your own tooling.
- Using the evaluation harness to measure your agents.

Sharing publicly is optional. If you would rather stay anonymous, an aggregate note
("a financial-services platform team") is welcome too.
